# Glossary - English to Bulgarian

The rule: **a programming term stays in English** when the Bulgarian word would hide
the English one the readers meet in documentation, error messages and code
(`variable`, not "променлива"; `array`, not "масив"). A term that is the same word in
both languages is written in Bulgarian (функция, обект, метод, оператор). Everyday
words are translated (стойност, условие, грешка).

## Stays in English

Written in Latin letters, without quotes or backticks. Plural in English
("variables", "arrays"). Avoid a Bulgarian article where you can ("в този array",
"елементите на array"); where you cannot, add it with a hyphen: "loop-ът",
"array-ят", "string-ът", "alias-а", "guard clause-ът".
